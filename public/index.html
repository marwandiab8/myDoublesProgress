Quick rules
  • Each double attempt is logged once: Hit on 1/2/3 or Miss.
  • If you miss a double, it stays unfinished and will be attempted again next round.
  • The app automatically cycles back to the earliest unfinished double until everything is closed.
  • Timer excludes paused time.
  • Undo last throw reverts your most recent hit or miss (including after a finished round removes the saved history row for that save).
