Quick rules
  • Each double attempt is logged once: Hit on 1/2/3 or Miss.
  • If you miss a double, it stays unfinished and will be attempted again next round.
  • The app automatically cycles back to the earliest unfinished double until everything is closed.
  • Timer excludes paused time.
  • Kept throwing at one double because it felt close? Log it under Stayed on this double (hit on dart 4 to 15, or missed after 6 to 15 darts). Every 3 darts count as an attempt, and the darts past the first 3 are shown as extra.
  • Missed a run of doubles, then hit one? Log it in one go under Hit a later double: pick the double and the total darts, even if the one you hit took more than 3. The doubles skipped over are recorded as misses (3 darts each).
  • Undo last throw reverts your most recent hit or miss (including after a finished round removes the saved history row for that save).
